DAEDAL Risers and Tension — free sound effects library by DAEDAL

WHAT'S INSIDE
  WAV, 48 kHz / 24-bit, stereo, peak -1 dBFS. File names describe the sound (character, length, direction:
  LR = left to right, RL = right to left). Every sound was synthesized from scratch — no samples, no AI.
  License: CC0 — free for any use, including client work. Credit appreciated, never required.

TIPS
  Whooshes: put the loudest point on the cut; layer with an impact for punch.
  Risers: end exactly on the cut or the beat drop; tension drones sit under suspense scenes at a low level.
  Impacts: "Boom" for titles and drops, "Punch" for fast cuts, "SubDrop" for reveals (needs headphones/subwoofer).
  UI sounds: keep them low (-18 to -12 dB) under music — they should be felt more than heard.

WHAT'S INSIDE
  Whooshes (8), Risers (6, incl. reverse cymbals), Impacts (5), UI clicks/pops/swipes (8),
  Glitches (6) and a seamless 16mm projector loop. WAV, 48 kHz / 24-bit, stereo, peak -1 dBFS.
  Every sound was synthesized from scratch (no samples, no AI). License: CC0 — free for any use.

TIPS
  - Whoosh: put its loudest point on the cut. Layer a whoosh + impact for punchy transitions.
  - Riser: end it exactly on the cut / beat drop, then hit an impact right after.
  - Reverse cymbals swell into the cut — great into a title or a music drop.
  - Projector loop: lay it under film-look sequences at a low level (-20 to -25 dB).
